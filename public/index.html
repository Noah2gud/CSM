Nofem Group Foundation

A member-owned cooperative where people save together, invest together, and grow together. Join us to build financial security and access real economic benefits.

Members contribute savings | Access investment opportunities | Receive economic benefits

Strength in Unity

We believe that when people come together with shared purpose, they can achieve what no individual can alone. Nofem Group Foundation brings together hardworking men and women who pool their savings, support each other, and build lasting wealth as a community.

Every contribution grows the pool that lifts every member.

Our Four Pillars

Everything we do is built on these foundations

Savings

Build financial security through disciplined weekly and voluntary savings. Your savings pool grows with every contribution from every member.

Investment

Members access agreed investment opportunities funded from the cooperative pool. Interest-free loans help you grow your business and income.

Land & Property

Cooperative members benefit from group access to land and property opportunities that would be difficult to reach individually.

Food Security

We support members in building food security through collective agricultural initiatives and shared resources within the community.

Grow Your Savings, Access Real Benefits

Members contribute compulsory weekly savings and voluntary savings, held securely in the cooperative pool. After 12 weeks of consistent contributions, you become eligible to apply for an interest-free loan of up to 2× your total savings.

Annual surpluses are shared among members. Your money works for you and for the collective.

How the Cooperative Works

Simple steps from joining to benefiting

1

Register

Apply for membership. Your details are verified and a credit check is run to confirm eligibility.

2

Pay Registration

Complete your one-time N5,000 registration fee to activate full membership.

3

Save Weekly

Contribute compulsory savings every week. Voluntary savings can be added at any time.

4

Access Loans

After 12 weeks, apply for an interest-free loan of up to 2× your total savings with two guarantors.

5

Earn Benefits

Share in the cooperative's annual surplus and access investment, property, and food security programmes.
